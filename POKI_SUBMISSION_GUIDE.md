### [Controls Guide] (조작법)
```text
Desktop Controls:
- Left Click: Tap generators (Food Pantry, Toy Box, First Aid Box) to produce items
- Click & Drag: Drag identical items together to merge into higher-level items
- Click 'Deliver' / 'Restore': Complete animal requests and upgrade shelter areas

Mobile / Touch Controls:
- Tap to generate items
- Drag & Drop with finger touch to merge items on the board
```
